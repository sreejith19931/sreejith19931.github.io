Rhythm

Designed for You

Dynamic Theming

Built with Material You. The entire interface transforms to complement the colors of your album artwork, creating a truly immersive experience.

🎚️

Precision Audio

A professional-grade 5-band equalizer with custom presets and environmental reverb controls to fine-tune every note.

✂️ Audio Trimmer

Craft the perfect ringtone or notification sound directly within the app. Select any part of your track and save it instantly.

🚗

Driving Mode

Stay focused on the road with a dedicated interface featuring large, accessible controls and high-contrast visuals.

🧹

Library Janitor

Keep your collection pristine. Automatically find missing artwork and repair messy metadata with a single tap.

📜

Smart Lyrics

Integrated support for both synced and static lyrics, ensuring you never miss a beat or a word.
