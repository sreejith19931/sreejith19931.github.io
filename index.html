Rhythm

An elegant, open-source music player designed for modern Android. High fidelity audio meets a UI that breathes with your music.

Get the APK Explore Source

Pure Performance

Built with Jetpack Compose and Media3 for lightning-fast responsiveness and flawless playback.

Total Privacy

No ads, no tracking, and no internet required. Your music belongs to you, and Rhythm keeps it that way.

Experience the Detail

Rhythm isn't just a player; it's a toolkit for the discerning listener.

🎨

Material You Theming

The entire interface transforms to complement the colors of your album artwork, creating a truly immersive visual experience that changes with every track.

🎚️

Studio-Grade EQ

A precision 5-band equalizer with custom presets, environmental reverb, and bass boost. Fine-tune your audio to match your headphones perfectly.

✂️

Precision Trimmer

Found the perfect hook? Use the built-in trimmer to create high-quality ringtones or notification clips directly from your local library.

🚗

Optimized Driving

A dedicated interface with large, tactile controls and high-contrast visuals designed for safe, distraction-free use while on the move.

🧹

Library Janitor

Automatically repair missing metadata and fetch high-resolution album art. Keep your collection organized and looking its absolute best.

📜

Seamless Lyrics

Dive deeper into your music with integrated support for both synced and static lyrics. Never miss a word of your favorite anthems.
